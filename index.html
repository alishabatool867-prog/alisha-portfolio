About me

Design is how I say things I can't say in words.

  • Based inPunjab, Pakistan
  • Working withClients worldwide
  • Available forFreelance & amp; contract

I'm Alisha — a creative digital freelancer who turns half-formed ideas into brands people remember. My work sits at the intersection of design, motion, and AI: I sketch a logo in the morning, cut a short-form video by lunch, and prompt-engineer a content system by evening.

I care about the details most people scroll past — the kerning, the frame timing, the exact shade of a brand's green. That attention is what makes a Canva post look designed, not templated, and a 30-second video feel intentional rather than assembled.

Every project starts with the same question: what does this brand need to feel like to earn trust in three seconds? Everything else — palette, pacing, prompts — follows from that answer.
